    Process

    A clear route from brief to delivery.

    Each stage creates a useful production artefact, keeping creative decisions visible and reviewable.

    1. 01

      Frame the brief

      Clarify audience, message, format, constraints, and the intended emotional read.

      Output: brief & reference route
    2. 02

      Build the visual route

      Shape the edit, styleframes, visual language, or AI-assisted exploration required by the project.

      Output: assembly & visual direction
    3. 03

      Review and refine

      Test pacing, clarity, motion, colour, and feedback against the agreed objective.

      Output: review versions
    4. 04

      Finish and deliver

      Complete quality control, format versions, and the final delivery package.

      Output: approved masters
